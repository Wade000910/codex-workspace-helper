# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being considerate, respectful, constructive, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently block participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer listed on the GitHub profile.
